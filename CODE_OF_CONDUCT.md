# Code of conduct 行为准则

GenTV is used by people with very different TVs, languages and levels of experience. Please keep issues, pull requests and discussions useful and kind.

GenTV 的用户来自不同国家，用着各种电视，经验也各不相同。请让 issue、PR 和讨论保持有用、友善。

## Expected 请这样做

- Be patient with people who are new to ADB or Android TV. 对刚接触 ADB 或 Android TV 的人保持耐心。
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume. 先假定对方是善意的，有疑问就问。
- Keep criticism about the work, with details someone can act on. 批评针对内容本身，并给出可以落实的细节。
- Report what you actually tested, and say when something wasn't tested. 只报告实际测过的结果，没测过的要说明。

## Not accepted 不接受的行为

- Harassment, insults, or attacks on someone's identity, background or language. 骚扰、辱骂，或针对他人身份、背景、语言的攻击。
- Sexual content, threats, or encouraging harm. 色情内容、威胁或鼓动伤害。
- Publishing someone else's private information, including TV addresses, ADB keys, account details or logs they haven't shared. 公开他人的隐私信息，包括电视地址、ADB 密钥、账号或对方没有公开的日志。
- Spam, advertising, or posting links to modified or repackaged GenTV builds. 垃圾信息、广告，或发布被修改、重新打包的 GenTV 安装包链接。

## Reporting 举报

Email [support@gentv.me](mailto:support@gentv.me) with a link to the comment or issue. Reports are kept confidential. Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

请发邮件到 [support@gentv.me](mailto:support@gentv.me)，附上相关评论或 issue 的链接，举报内容会保密。安全问题请按 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 私下报告。

## Enforcement 处理方式

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, or block accounts that break these rules. A first problem usually gets a private reminder; repeated or serious problems lead to a block.

维护者可以编辑或删除违规内容、锁定讨论，或屏蔽违规账号。初次问题通常会私下提醒，多次或严重违规会被屏蔽。

These rules apply in this repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and in GenTV's official channels.

本准则适用于本仓库及其 issue、PR、讨论区，以及 GenTV 的官方渠道。
